# Filament Resources Blueprint

## Overview
Houses all Filament administrative CMS resources managing domain models, forms, tables, and relation managers under the `/console` panel.

## Subdirectories & Boundaries
- [ArCreatives](./ArCreatives/EGM.md): Augmented reality creatives, MindAR compiled files, and video assets.
- [Areas](./Areas/EGM.md): Geographic sales territories and doctor area groupings.
- [ContactSubmissions](./ContactSubmissions/EGM.md): Inbound website leads and contact inquiries inbox.
- [DCRS](./DCRS/EGM.md): Medical Representative Daily Call Reports inspection.
- [Doctors](./Doctors/EGM.md): Healthcare provider master directory and geolocation mapping.
- [Headquarters](./Headquarters/EGM.md): Regional sales headquarters.
- [Microsites](./Microsites/EGM.md): Personalized doctor landing pages (`/dr/{slug}`).
- [NewsPosts](./NewsPosts/EGM.md): Company press releases and medical news CMS.
- [ProductPricings](./ProductPricings/EGM.md): Multi-tier wholesale, retail, and stockist pricing.
- [Products](./Products/EGM.md): Pharmaceutical product formulations and pack shots catalog.
- [PromotionalInputs](./PromotionalInputs/EGM.md): Marketing collateral and doctor sample inputs.
- [QrCodes](./QrCodes/EGM.md): Dynamic QR codes and real-time scan telemetry analytics.
- [TherapeuticAreas](./TherapeuticAreas/EGM.md): Clinical therapeutic categories.
- [Users](./Users/EGM.md): Administrative and field user accounts.
