---

## Overview

The Doctor Commercial Performance & ROI Tracker is an administrative decision-support interface embedded inside the Exponit Labs Filament v5 console (`/console`). It calculates and visualizes the net commercial return generated by the company's 233 registered healthcare providers relative to the operational capital invested in detailing visits, drug samples, and promotional gifts.

By correlating Daily Call Reports (DCRs), distributed product samples, and promotional collateral with monthly prescription revenue audits, the system categorizes physicians into deterministic commercial performance tiers:
* **Incubation:** Newly registered physicians undergoing initial relationship ramp-up (<60 days).
* **High-Yield Prescribers (Net Positive):** Commercial prescription revenue exceeds total detailing expenditure by 50% or more.
* **Balanced Prescribers:** Prescriptions generate positive margin covering detailing costs, but remain below the 50% ROI growth target.
* **Under-Yielding Prescribers (Net Negative):** Detailing, sampling, and promotional expenditures exceed attributed prescription revenue.

---

## Problem Statement

Exponit Labs deploys field Medical Representatives (MRs) across target territories, incurring recurring expenses on daily clinic visits, commercial medicine samples, and promotional gifts. 

Currently:
1. **Activity logs lack cost attribution:** Daily Call Reports (`d_c_r_s`), drug samples (`dcr_products`), and promotional items (`dcr_promotional_inputs`) record rep activities, but lack financial valuation.
2. **Revenue is disconnected:** Secondary prescription sales flow through distributors and retail stockists without being attributed back to prescribing doctors in the primary database.
3. **Resource allocation lacks profitability data:** Sales managers cannot determine which high-frequency doctors fail to generate reciprocal commercial returns, leading to misallocated field force travel time and unmonitored sampling spend.

## Goals & Non-Goals

### Goals
1. **Account-Level Detailing Valuation:** Calculate total capital deployed per doctor by aggregating:
   - Call visit expenses (baseline site-wide cost per call, with optional doctor-level override).
   - Commercial drug samples handed over (quantity $\times$ sample unit cost).
   - Promotional collateral and gifts delivered (quantity $\times$ promotional input unit cost).
2. **Attributed Revenue Ingestion:** Provide an idempotent monthly prescription revenue ingestion mechanism (`doctor_revenues`) supporting single-record modal entry and CSV batch imports in Filament.
3. **Management Performance Dashboard:** Provide a Filament v5 management page at [`/console/doctor-portfolio`](file:///Users/arm/Herd/exponitlabs/app/Filament/Pages/DoctorPortfolio.php) displaying:
   - Portfolio summary metrics (Total Capital Invested, Attributed Revenue, Net Commercial P&L, and Overall Return Multiple).
   - Filterable, sortable prescriber ledger with color-coded Net P&L (₹) and ROI status tiers.
   - Calendar-aligned accounting period filters (`This Financial Year`, `Last 3 Months`, `Last Closed Month`, `All Time`).
4. **SQLite Query Efficiency:** Execute all aggregations in database queries via correlated subqueries and composite indexes wrapped in a derived query, preventing N+1 execution bottlenecks and enabling native database sorting.
5. **Division-by-Zero Prevention:** Ensure metrics evaluate cleanly without `DivisionByZeroError` exceptions when assessing accounts with zero historical expenditure or zero revenue.

### Non-Goals
1. **Direct Live ERPNext Database Sync (v1):** Direct MariaDB socket connections or synchronous API calls into local [Frappe/ERPNext](https://frappe.io/erpnext) instances are excluded for v1. Revenue data remains native to Laravel's database; automated ERP synchronization is postponed to a v2 background queue importer.
2. **Mobile Field App (`/mr`) Exposure:** Field representatives will not see financial margins, doctor P&L, or return tiers on their mobile devices. Detailing activity entry on `/mr` remains unaffected.
3. **Standalone UI Frameworks:** No separate Single Page Application or custom charting ribbons outside Filament. All views utilize standard Filament v5 tables, schemas, and stats widgets.

### CUJ 1: Sales Director Triages Under-Yielding Accounts
* **Given**: A sales director is reviewing territory performance in `/console/doctor-portfolio`.
* **When**: The director selects "This Financial Year" and sorts the table by Net P&L ascending.
* **Then**: The top of the table highlights doctors with high call frequency and high sample consumption who have generated minimal or zero attributed revenue, allowing management to reduce rep call frequency on those accounts.

* **When**: The manager clicks "Upload Revenue CSV" on the portfolio page, selects the October 2026 fallback period, and uploads a validated CSV file.
* **Then**: The system validates doctor IDs in memory, commits valid revenue records in a single sub-20ms transaction, logs the import batch, and updates portfolio Net P&L across all dashboard cards without duplicate key collisions.

* **Given**: The price of promotional kits has changed and average visit travel allowances have been revised.
* **When**: The admin updates the unit cost on `PromotionalInputResource` and adjusts the default visit cost setting.
* **Then**: New DCR activities snapshot the updated cost baselines immediately, while past closed quarters retain their historical cost snapshots.

### 3. Financial Calculation Formulations

For any given doctor $d$ and selected date window $[T_{\text{start}}, T_{\text{end}}]$:

$$\text{Effective Visit Cost} = d.\text{visit\_cost\_override} \mathbin{??} \text{config}('exponit.default\_visit\_cost', 250.00)$$

$$\text{Visit Investment} = \text{Count}(\text{DCRs in } [T_{\text{start}}, T_{\text{end}}]) \times \text{Effective Visit Cost}$$

$$\text{Sample Investment} = \sum_{\text{dcr\_products}} (\text{quantity} \times \text{COALESCE}(\text{unit\_cost\_snapshot}, \text{products.sample\_cost}, \text{products.unit\_cost}, 0.00))$$

$$\text{Promotional Investment} = \sum_{\text{dcr\_promotional\_inputs}} (\text{quantity} \times \text{COALESCE}(\text{unit\_cost\_snapshot}, \text{promotional\_inputs.unit\_cost}, 0.00))$$

$$\text{Total Invested Capital} = \text{Visit Investment} + \text{Sample Investment} + \text{Promotional Investment}$$

$$\text{Total Attributed Revenue} = \sum_{\text{doctor\_revenues in } [T_{\text{revenue\_start}}, T_{\text{end}}]} \text{amount}$$
*(where $T_{\text{revenue\_start}} = T_{\text{start}}.\text{startOfMonth()}$)*

$$\text{Net Commercial P\&L} = \text{Total Attributed Revenue} - \text{Total Invested Capital}$$

$$\text{ROI \%} = \begin{cases} 
\text{null / "Organic"} & \text{if } \text{Total Invested Capital} = 0 \text{ and Revenue} > 0 \\
0.0\% & \text{if } \text{Total Invested Capital} = 0 \text{ and Revenue} = 0 \\
-100.0\% & \text{if } \text{Total Invested Capital} > 0 \text{ and Revenue} = 0 \\
\frac{\text{Net Commercial P\&L}}{\text{Total Invested Capital}} \times 100 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}$$

$$\text{Overall Return Multiple} = \begin{cases}
0.0\text{x} & \text{if } \text{Total Invested Capital} = 0 \\
\frac{\text{Total Attributed Revenue}}{\text{Total Invested Capital}}\text{x} & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}$$

#### Performance Tier Classification Logic
Each physician is classified into exactly one deterministic tier evaluated in strict precedence order:
1. **Incubation** (Badge: Info / Gray):
   - *Condition:* `doctors.created_at >= now()->subDays(60)`
   - *Rationale:* New physicians undergoing relationship ramp-up are exempted from profitability scoring during their initial 60 days.
2. **High-Yield Prescriber** (Badge: Success / Green):
   - *Condition:* `net_pnl > 0` AND `roi_percentage >= 50.0`
   - *Rationale:* Accounts where commercial sales exceed total invested detailing capital by 50% or more.
3. **Balanced Prescriber** (Badge: Warning / Amber):
   - *Condition:* `net_pnl >= 0` AND `roi_percentage < 50.0`
   - *Rationale:* Accounts generating positive net margin that covers ongoing visit and detailing costs but remains below the 50% ROI target.
4. **Under-Yielding Prescriber** (Badge: Danger / Red):
   - *Condition:* `net_pnl < 0`
   - *Rationale:* Accounts where promotional inputs, samples, and visit expenditures exceed attributed prescription revenue.

### 4. SQLite Query Service (`DoctorPortfolioQueryService`)

To prevent N+1 query loops and permit native database sorting on `net_pnl` and `total_investment` in SQLite, the query service builds an inner query with correlated subqueries and wraps it in a derived `fromSub()` projection. This projects real SQL columns that Filament's `orderBy()` can sort directly:

```php
namespace App\Services;

use App\Models\DCR;
use App\Models\Doctor;
use App\Models\DoctorRevenue;
use Illuminate\Database\Eloquent\Builder;
use Illuminate\Support\Carbon;
use Illuminate\Support\Facades\DB;

class DoctorPortfolioQueryService
{
    public function getBaseQuery(?Carbon $startDate = null, ?Carbon $endDate = null): Builder
    {
        $defaultVisitCost = (float) config('exponit.default_visit_cost', 250.00);

        // Snap revenue start date to beginning of month to prevent rolling date exclusion
        $revenueStartDate = $startDate ? $startDate->copy()->startOfMonth() : null;

        $visitsQuery = DCR::query()
            ->selectRaw('COUNT(*)')
            ->whereColumn('d_c_r_s.doctor_id', 'doctors.id')
            ->when($startDate, fn ($q) => $q->where('d_c_r_s.date', '>=', $startDate))
            ->when($endDate, fn ($q) => $q->where('d_c_r_s.date', '<=', $endDate));

        $samplesQuery = DB::table('dcr_products')
            ->join('d_c_r_s', 'd_c_r_s.id', '=', 'dcr_products.dcr_id')
            ->join('products', 'products.id', '=', 'dcr_products.product_id')
            ->selectRaw('COALESCE(SUM(dcr_products.quantity * COALESCE(dcr_products.unit_cost_snapshot, products.sample_cost, products.unit_cost, 0.00)), 0)')
            ->whereColumn('d_c_r_s.doctor_id', 'doctors.id')
            ->when($startDate, fn ($q) => $q->where('d_c_r_s.date', '>=', $startDate))
            ->when($endDate, fn ($q) => $q->where('d_c_r_s.date', '<=', $endDate));

        $promosQuery = DB::table('dcr_promotional_inputs')
            ->join('d_c_r_s', 'd_c_r_s.id', '=', 'dcr_promotional_inputs.dcr_id')
            ->join('promotional_inputs', 'promotional_inputs.id', '=', 'dcr_promotional_inputs.promotional_input_id')
            ->selectRaw('COALESCE(SUM(dcr_promotional_inputs.quantity * COALESCE(dcr_promotional_inputs.unit_cost_snapshot, promotional_inputs.unit_cost, 0.00)), 0)')
            ->whereColumn('d_c_r_s.doctor_id', 'doctors.id')
            ->when($startDate, fn ($q) => $q->where('d_c_r_s.date', '>=', $startDate))
            ->when($endDate, fn ($q) => $q->where('d_c_r_s.date', '<=', $endDate));

        $revenueQuery = DoctorRevenue::query()
            ->selectRaw('COALESCE(SUM(doctor_revenues.amount), 0)')
            ->whereColumn('doctor_revenues.doctor_id', 'doctors.id')
            ->when($revenueStartDate, fn ($q) => $q->where('doctor_revenues.period_date', '>=', $revenueStartDate))
            ->when($endDate, fn ($q) => $q->where('doctor_revenues.period_date', '<=', $endDate));

        $innerQuery = Doctor::query()
            ->select('doctors.*')
            ->selectSub($visitsQuery, 'visits_count')
            ->selectSub($samplesQuery, 'samples_cost')
            ->selectSub($promosQuery, 'promos_cost')
            ->selectSub($revenueQuery, 'total_revenue');

        return Doctor::query()
            ->fromSub($innerQuery, 'doctors')
            ->select('doctors.*')
            ->selectRaw('((doctors.visits_count * COALESCE(doctors.visit_cost_override, ?)) + doctors.samples_cost + doctors.promos_cost) AS total_investment', [$defaultVisitCost])
            ->selectRaw('(doctors.total_revenue - ((doctors.visits_count * COALESCE(doctors.visit_cost_override, ?)) + doctors.samples_cost + doctors.promos_cost)) AS net_pnl', [$defaultVisitCost]);
    }

    /**
     * Resolve start and end Carbon timestamps for predefined accounting horizons.
     *
     * @return array{0: ?Carbon, 1: ?Carbon}
     */
    public function resolveHorizonRange(string $horizon): array
    {
        $now = Carbon::now();

        return match ($horizon) {
            'current_fy' => [
                $now->month >= 4 
                    ? Carbon::create($now->year, 4, 1)->startOfDay() 
                    : Carbon::create($now->year - 1, 4, 1)->startOfDay(),
                $now->month >= 4 
                    ? Carbon::create($now->year + 1, 3, 31)->endOfDay() 
                    : Carbon::create($now->year, 3, 31)->endOfDay(),
            ],
            'last_3_months' => [
                $now->copy()->subMonths(3)->startOfMonth(),
                $now->endOfDay(),
            ],
            'last_month' => [
                $now->copy()->subMonth()->startOfMonth(),
                $now->copy()->subMonth()->endOfMonth(),
            ],
            default => [null, null], // all_time
        };
    }
}
```

## Risks & Mitigations

### 1. SQLite Database Concurrency & Bulk Ingestion Locks
* **Risk**: SQLite operates with a database-level write lock. A large monthly revenue CSV import could hold an exclusive lock, causing `database is locked` errors for mobile field reps submitting DCRs via `/mr`.
* **Mitigation**: With SQLite WAL (Write-Ahead Logging) mode enabled in Laravel Herd, read queries execute concurrently without blocking writes. The `DoctorRevenueImportService` performs in-memory CSV validation first and commits all valid rows in a single atomic transaction executing `DoctorRevenue::insert()` in under 20ms for 250 rows, eliminating long lock windows.

### 2. Healthcare Compliance (UCPMP) & Commercial Privacy
* **Risk**: Correlating physician prescribing revenue with promotional input expenditure and detailing visits is legally sensitive under India's Uniform Code for Pharmaceuticals Marketing Practices ([UCPMP](https://www.pharmaceuticals.gov.in)). Exposing account profitability to unauthorized staff creates compliance and reputational risk.
* **Mitigation**: Access to `/console/doctor-portfolio` is restricted via Filament page authorization (`canAccess()`), limiting visibility strictly to executive and administrative roles (`SuperAdmin`, `SalesDirector`). The mobile field force application (`/mr`) has zero access to this page or financial calculations.

### 3. Historical P&L Corruption via Price Changes
* **Risk**: Modifying a promotional gift or product cost in the master catalog could retroactively alter closed quarterly and annual P&L statements.
* **Mitigation**: Add a nullable `unit_cost_snapshot` (`decimal:10,2`) column to `dcr_products` and `dcr_promotional_inputs`. When logging a DCR, the active catalog price is snapshotted. The query engine evaluates `COALESCE(unit_cost_snapshot, live_cost, 0.00)`, protecting historical accounting while allowing legacy records without snapshots to resolve cleanly.

### 4. Temporal Alignment Between Monthly Revenues and Daily Logs
* **Risk**: Daily DCR logs combined with monthly normalized revenues (`YYYY-MM-01`) create artificial deficits when applying rolling intra-month filters.
* **Mitigation**: Replace rolling daily windows with calendar-aligned filters (`last_3_months`, `current_fy`, `last_month`, `all_time`), and clamp query start dates for monthly revenue to `startOfMonth()`.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative A: Direct ERPNext Database Connection (MariaDB Socket Link)
* **Pros**: Direct access to raw sales invoice entries stored in the local Frappe ERPNext system without requiring separate manual uploads.
* **Cons**:
  1. *Cross-Stack Coupling:* Tying Laravel's operational console to an external local MariaDB instance creates multi-stack dependencies (Python/Bench vs. PHP/Herd). If the MariaDB service is halted, `/console` encounters fatal PDO connection errors.
  2. *Domain Mismatch (Stockists vs. Doctors):* In pharmaceutical ERPs, sales invoices are issued to commercial distributors, stockists, and retail chemists—not prescribing doctors. ERPNext does not track physician prescriptions without custom tracking.
* **Reason for Rejection**: Fails to resolve doctor attribution and introduces operational fragility. Decoupling revenue into a native SQLite table with a future v2 asynchronous API importer is cleaner and more reliable.

### Alternative B: Standalone SPA Frontend with Broker-Terminal Styling
* **Pros**: Complete aesthetic freedom to build custom high-density stock-broker terminal layouts and animations.
* **Cons**:
  1. *Infrastructure Duplication:* Requires duplicate authentication, routing, and deployment infrastructure outside the established `/console` Filament CMS.
  2. *Ethical & Regulatory Risks:* Framing physicians with stock tickers, "gainers vs. losers", and "capital drains" introduces cognitive friction and violates pharmaceutical marketing compliance norms (e.g., UCPMP in India) if reports are shared or audited.
  3. *Maintenance Overhead:* Diverges from Filament v5's design system and breaks native accessibility and responsive table controls.
* **Reason for Rejection**: Native Filament v5 components (`StatsOverviewWidget`, Table filters, and badges) satisfy business requirements with zero redundant framework overhead.

### Alternative C: Dynamic Eloquent Model Accessors (`$doctor->total_investment`)
* **Pros**: Rapid to write using standard Laravel Eloquent relationship helpers.
* **Cons**:
  1. *N+1 Query Roundtrips:* Calculating totals in PHP accessors requires executing 4 queries per doctor row. At 50 doctors per page, that is 201 database roundtrips per request.
  2. *Pagination & Sorting Failure:* Filament Table pagination cannot sort on PHP-computed virtual accessors in SQL without loading every doctor in the database into memory.
* **Reason for Rejection**: High query latency and pagination breakdown. Correlated SQL subqueries in `DoctorPortfolioQueryService` execute in <15ms on SQLite and enable native `ORDER BY` sorting and pagination.

### Alternative D: Materialized Snapshot Roll-up Tables / Background Cron Aggregation
* **Pros**: Pre-aggregates daily metrics into a dedicated summary table (`doctor_roi_snapshots`), simplifying read queries.
* **Cons**:
  1. *Unnecessary Complexity:* The Exponit Labs database currently has 233 doctors and under 1,000 DCR records.
  2. *State Synchronization Lag:* Introduces cache invalidation bugs, delayed updates, and cron job scheduling overhead.
* **Reason for Rejection**: Unnecessary overhead. Correlated SQL subqueries on indexed SQLite tables run in under 15ms, eliminating the need for materialized caches in v1.
